01 / DECLARE THE FRAME LAW

Nothing hidden in the logits.

2-4 unique printable ASCII symbols. Blank is an index, not a special spelling. 0-6 frames, each an exact rational probability row. Beam width 1-64. No softmax, language model or length bonus.

Controls were selected in development, not held out. No speech model, recognition quality or calibrated confidence claim.

Editing clears old evidence and disables download. Duplicate keys, JSON float/exponent tokens and noncanonical fractions are refused.

02 / THREE DIFFERENT QUESTIONS

A path. A beam. The whole law.

Exact transcript mass sums all compatible independent-frame paths. The beam score sums only paths whose collapsed prefixes survived every pruning cut. It is not renormalized.

04 / A SUFFICIENT BOUND, NOT A PROMISE

Can lost mass change the winner?

Let L be the chosen transcript's retained mass, R the largest retained mass of any other final transcript (zero for unseen ones), and D the total discarded mass. Every other transcript has full mass at most R + D. If L > R + D, this is the unique MAP transcript of the supplied law.

This strict bound is sufficient, not necessary. A false result can accompany a correct answer. Exact ties never receive a uniqueness certificate. No coverage, confidence, acoustic correctness or recognition guarantee follows.

06 / KEEP THE CLAIM SMALL

A search audit, not a recognizer.

This implements established CTC prefix decoding with exact rational supplied probabilities. A wider finite beam can change the surviving path set in either direction. No monotonic finite-width quality or speed claim. All mass accounting assumes the supplied rows form independent categorical frames.

The native checker enumerates frame paths independently of the generator's prefix recurrence. Replay checks consistency, not authenticity, a model's calibration or the truth of a transcript. At most 4,096 complete paths: this is an evidence inspector, not a scalable decoder.
